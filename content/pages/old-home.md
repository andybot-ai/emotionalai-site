# **Exploring what it means to live with emotionally responsive AI**

Founded in 2017, the Emotional AI Lab examines the social, cultural, legal and ethical impact of artificial intelligence technologies that function in relation to data about human emotions, moods, affective states, psychological disposition, and other intimate dimensions of human life.

If you’re looking for our academic and policy work, check all tabs, but especially ‘[Outputs](https://emotionalai.org/outputs)’.

If you’re interested in collaboration, so are we :)

For info and media input contact [Prof. Andrew McStay](mailto:mcstay@bangor.ac.uk) and he’ll answer or connect you with the right person from [the team](https://emotionalai.org/who).
