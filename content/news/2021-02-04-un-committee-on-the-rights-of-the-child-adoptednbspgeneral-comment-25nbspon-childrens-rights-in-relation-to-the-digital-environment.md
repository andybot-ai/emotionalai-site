On February 4th 2021 the UN Committee on the Rights of the Child adopted [General Comment 25](https://tbinternet.ohchr.org/_layouts/15/treatybodyexternal/TBSearch.aspx?TreatyID=5&DocTypeID=11) on children’s rights in relation to the digital environment. As [Prof. Sonia Livingstone explains](https://blogs.lse.ac.uk/medialse/2021/02/04/childrens-rights-apply-in-the-digital-world/), ‘it’s a game changer’ in that the General Comment ’will land on the desk of every government in the world,’ with each being duty-bound to the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child.

From the point of view of the Lab, we’re thrilled. We submitted an extended [Comment](https://www.ohchr.org/Documents/HRBodies/CRC/GCChildrensDigitalEnvironment/2020/others/emotional-ai-lab-2020-11-11.docx) on the need to recognise that profiling of children’s emotional life is profoundly problematic. Sections 17, 63 and 64 of [General Comment 25](https://tbinternet.ohchr.org/_layouts/15/treatybodyexternal/TBSearch.aspx?TreatyID=5&DocTypeID=11) seem to have taken this onboard.

For sure, this is not the end, but it’s an *excellent* start!
