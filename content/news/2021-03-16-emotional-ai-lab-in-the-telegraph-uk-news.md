Andrew McStay was interviewed for a Telegraph article that explored ‘tyranny in the woke workplace.’ A rhetorical device for sure, to hook politically conservative readers, it’s a good article that investigates biometrics and tone policing at work. Article [here](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/business/2021/03/12/hr-departments-impose-tech-tyranny-woke-workplace/).

Worth noting too that the Lab routinely finds that older folk (that the Telegraph appeals to) really do not like the idea of *any* profiling of emotion and bodily behaviour. Concern about the social impact of these technologies is an issue that cuts across age groups and the political matrix.
