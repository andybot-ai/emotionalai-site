Andrew McStay and Vian Bakir have been awarded [Flemish Center Data and Society Fellowships](https://www.law.kuleuven.be/citip/en/news/item/cds-citip-fellow-lectures-series-tackling-grand-challenges-in-society-artificial-intelligence ) to spend time with the Centre for IT & IP Law (CITIP) at the University of Leuven in Belgium. Among other activities, they will be giving *Tackling Grand Challenges in Society* talks under the auspice of the Joint Research Training Program 2019-2020 between the Law Schools of KU Leuven & University of Edinburgh.

Want to come?

Date & time: Tuesday, December 17, 16h-18h30

Location: COVE 02.10, Romerozaal (2nd floor), Sint- Michielsstraat 6, 3000 Leuven.

Participation is free but registration is required via law.citip.admin@kuleuven.be (before December 16th)
