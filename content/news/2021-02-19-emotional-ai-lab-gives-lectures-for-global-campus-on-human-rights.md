Fellow lecturers include **Edward Snowden,** President of the Freedom of the Press Foundation and Right Livelihood Laureate; **Joe Cannataci**, UN Special Rapporteur on the right to privacy; **Mary Jo Madda**, Google; **Emma Day**, Human Rights Lawyer and Child Protection Consultant; **Marianne Díaz Hernández**, human rights activist and policy analyst for Derechos Digitales; **Neha Gauchan**, Program Officer of ChildSafeNet Nepal; **Bertalan Meskó**, Director, The Medical Futurist Institute, among brilliant others. Andrew gave two sessions, one on the role of emotional AI in education, and another on AI, emotion and toys.
